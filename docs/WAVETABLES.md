# Wavetables and spectral waves

The Virus's "wave select" (63 spectral waves) and the TI's wavetables are Access's data. fm1-hv ships none of it.

- Built in: wave 0 is a sine, wave 1 a triangle, waves 2..63 are fm1-hv's own generated spectra, so a patch that
  selects a spectral wave still sounds.
- Your own: phase 4 adds an import (in the browser editor) of single-cycle or multi-frame WAV wavetables from your
  own files, for example the Virus waveforms Adam Szabo publishes for his Viper with Access Music's permission.
  They go into your FM-1's flash, downsampled to fit; they are never part of an fm1-hv package.
